Dataset Label Definitions

The dataset uses a binary target definition for intrusion-related alarm detection.

Binary classes
--------------

positive
    Intrusion-related alarm event. The recording contains unauthorized room entry
    or an intrusion-related sequence associated with access to the protected room.

negative
    Non-intrusion or nuisance event. The recording does not contain unauthorized
    room entry and is intended to represent challenging non-alarm conditions.

Primary actions
---------------

door_entry
    Entry into the protected room through the door.

window_entry
    Entry into the protected room through the window.

room_entry
    Entry into the monitored room involving an interior/rear-side access path,
    or a sequence involving more than one access path.

access_attempt
    Interaction with the door, handle, key, or lock without successful entry.

external_alarm
    External alarm or siren-like acoustic event without intrusion.

ambient_noise
    Environmental or background noise without intrusion.

thrown_object
    An object intentionally thrown within or near the monitored region.

falling_object
    An object intentionally released and allowed to fall.

Access point
------------

door
    Entry or interaction occurs at the door.

window
    Entry occurs through the window.

rear_side
    Entry originates from the rear side of the sensor relative to its main
    monitored region.

rear_side, door
    The recording contains entry activity involving both the rear-side region
    and the door.

none
    No intrusion access point is involved.

Motion style
------------

normal
    Normal walking or movement speed.

slow
    Deliberately slow movement.

fast
    Rapid movement or running.

stealth
    Discreet, crouched, quiet, or otherwise stealth-like movement.

slow_fast
    The recording contains both slow and fast entry movements.

none
    No intruder movement is present.

Speech
------

yes
    Human speech is present as part of the intrusion-related recording.

no
    No intrusion-related speech is present.

Additional disturbances
-----------------------

none
    No additional disturbance was intentionally included.

door_knock
    Knocking on the door.

door_impact
    Strong impact or hitting of the door.

repeated_entry
    The same intruder performs more than one room-entry event within the recording.

ambient_noise
    General environmental background noise.

construction_noise
    Construction-related background noise.

air_conditioner_noise
    Air-conditioner background noise.

rain_noise
    Rain-related environmental noise.

external_siren
    External siren-like sound occurring during the recording.

siren
    Siren-like sound used as the primary negative acoustic event.

falling_object
    An object is allowed to fall during an otherwise positive recording.

object_fall
    Object-fall event used as the primary negative event.

thrown_object
    An intentionally thrown object occurring as an additional event.

object_impact
    Acoustic/mechanical impact produced by an object.

cardboard_object
    Falling-object event involving a cardboard object.

metal_object
    Falling-object event involving a metallic object.

forced_entry
    Forced entry through the monitored access point.

Annotation files
----------------

Each recording folder contains:

Radar-Data.data
    Radar macro-detection data acquired at 10 Hz.

Microphone-Data.wav
    Stereo audio acquired at 16 kHz.

labelf.label
    Event annotation file.

The label file contains the following fields:

Time(Seconds)
    Start time of the annotated interval.

Length(Seconds)
    Duration of the annotated interval.

Label(string)
    Event label assigned to the interval.

Confidence(double)
    Annotation confidence value.

Comment(string)
    Optional annotation comment.

The recording-level class describes the overall scenario. Individual nuisance
events may therefore occur inside a positive recording. For example, a positive
intrusion recording may also contain a falling object, door impact, external
noise, or another disturbance. Such additional events are described separately
in sessions.csv and do not replace the recording-level intrusion label.
